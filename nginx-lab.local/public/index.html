About

Patience is the only technique that matters.

Based in Hamburg, I work across editorial, documentary, and personal projects. My practice is rooted in the belief that the camera is most useful when the photographer is least visible.

Clients include magazines, NGOs, and private collectors across Europe. I'm available for long-term assignments and residencies.
